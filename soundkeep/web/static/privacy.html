Privacy Policy

Effective July 17, 2026 \u00b7 SoundKeep \u00b7 Powered by vLOID Technologies

What SoundKeep stores

SoundKeep works without accounts. Your library, crates, saved pathways, play history, theme, and playback-source preference are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not operate user accounts, and this locally stored data is not transmitted to our servers.

What our servers see

When you search or generate a pathway, your query is processed by the SoundKeep service to return results, using standard server logs (such as IP address and request data) for operation and security. Track metadata is derived from our seed catalog and the Last.fm API.

Third parties

Play buttons open YouTube, Spotify, or Apple Music in a new tab based on your chosen playback source. Those services apply their own terms and privacy policies once you are there. SoundKeep does not stream, host, or store audio files.

Analytics and advertising

SoundKeep currently uses no third-party advertising and no cross-site tracking.

Data removal

Clearing your browser's site data for SoundKeep removes everything stored locally. Nothing else is retained about you.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated as features such as accounts and SK token staking launch, and material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
