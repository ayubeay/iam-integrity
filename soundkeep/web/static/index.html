How It Works

What Sonic does for you

Not random recommendations. Intentional pathways with reasoning behind every step.

01
🔍
Discover
Start from any artist, track, or genre. Sonic queries millions of tracks to find your anchor point instantly — no account needed.
02
🗺
Map the Pathway
Generate an intelligent continuation — not "people who liked X also liked Y", but reasoned transitions based on genre, energy, region, era, and set position.
03
▶️
Listen Anywhere
Every recommendation links out to YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Music. SoundKeep decides what plays next. You choose where.
The Difference

Built for flow, not algorithms

Every pathway decision is explainable. Sonic shows you why — not just what.

🎸
Genre-aware
Understands Afrobeats, Dancehall, Hip-Hop, R&B, Soca, Amapiano, and the bridges between them.
⚡
Energy-aware
Tracks energy level across the set — warm-up, build, peak, wind-down — and matches accordingly.
🌍
Regional awareness
Knows the difference between Nigerian Afrobeats and Jamaican Dancehall. Regional bridges feel natural, not forced.
📅
Era matching
Classic to contemporary, 2000s to 2020s — Sonic keeps your pathway temporally coherent.
🎛
Set position logic
Knows when to stay in warm-up, when to build, when to peak, and when to wind down. Designed with DJs in mind.
🔗
Artist relationships
Understands which artists bridge naturally — Afrobeats into Dancehall, Hip-Hop into R&B — and explains every connection.
