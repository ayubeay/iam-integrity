# SOUNDKEEP — Emotional Library Reserve
**Status:** RESERVED · **Reserved:** July 19, 2026 (committed July 21)
**Architecture note:** Deliberately separate from the Vault reserves — the Vault is ownership and preservation; this is meaning and memory.

# Core Thesis
Music is remembered emotionally before it is remembered technically. People say "This song saved me," "This reminds me of my childhood," "The vocals give me goosebumps" — not bitrate and release year. SoundKeep preserves these emotional relationships alongside the music.

# Product Philosophy
Every song has two identities. Technical: artist, album, BPM, key, genre, duration. Human: memory, feeling, story, inspiration, personal significance. Both deserve preservation.

# Emotional Collections
Beyond playlists, collections centered on experience: Sounds Like Magic, Goosebumps, Songs That Changed My Life, Late Night, Rainy Days, Studio Inspiration, Healing, Motivation, Childhood Memories, First Love, Road Trips. Never limited by genre.

# Personal Story Layer
Per song, optional: why this matters to me, where I first heard it, who introduced me, associated memory, favorite lyric/moment/instrument/production detail. The emotional context belongs to the listener.

# Creator Feedback
Artists eventually receive qualitative insight, not only numbers: listeners often mention vocal harmonies, emotional lyrics, cinematic atmosphere, production quality, bassline, guitar tone, storytelling. Understanding, not vanity metrics.

# AI Understanding
Future systems learn emotional patterns (hope, nostalgia, calm, confidence, healing, motivation). Recommendations eventually combine technical similarity AND emotional similarity.

# Music Timeline
Meaningful interactions form a personal musical timeline: added to library > added to "Sounds Like Magic" > shared with friend > played 500 times > wedding playlist > inspired new project > referenced in journal. The song becomes part of a life story.

# Vault Relationship
The Permanent Vault preserves the music; this reserve preserves the meaning attached to it. Both travel together.

# Privacy
Emotional notes belong to the user. Default private; optionally shared with friends, collaborators, or publicly. Users remain in complete control.

# Product Doctrine
SoundKeep never reduces music to files. Music carries memories, emotions, inspiration, identity, relationships, creativity — the library preserves all of them.

# Long-Term Vision
Traditional libraries organize songs; SoundKeep organizes musical lives — searchable by feelings, memories, inspiration, creative impact, personal history. A living musical memory that grows with every listener and creator.
