# RESERVE - Technical Due Diligence & Founder Match Layer

**Status:** Reserved for future exploration.
**Urgency:** LOW as a product, with one immediate personal use.

## Purpose
A governed technical due-diligence layer helping non-technical founders and technical
builders decide whether to work together before equity, money or code change hands.

Not another founder marketplace. The execution-trust layer above them.

## Problem
Founder matching evaluates resumes, LinkedIn, portfolios, introductions and interviews.
None of that answers: can this founder execute? Is the architecture legitimate? Does the
MVP exist? Were customers actually validated? Are metrics reproducible? Does the codebase
match the claim? Has technical debt already accumulated? Is the equity split proportional
to contribution?

## Evidence categories
**Founder** identity, execution history, shipped products, funding, customer validation,
operating history. **Product** architecture, repository, deployment verification,
scalability, security, documentation, technical debt. **Business** customer interviews,
revenue, contracts, usage, retention, churn. **Collaboration** responsibilities, ownership
map, equity assumptions, decision rights, IP, vesting readiness.

## Receipts
Architecture Review, Repository Health, Customer Validation, Technical Debt, Founder
Contribution, Execution History, Equity Readiness, Collaboration Readiness.

## Integration
VERITY trust scoring, IAM verified identity, OROS workflow, Shield Router fraud prevention,
DRIFT execution drift after a partnership starts, KONIGO Connect continuity across
organisations and repositories.

## Grading
Low urgency as a product. But there is an immediate personal use: the same questions apply
in reverse when someone approaches you. The trader friend interested in contributing, and
any future collaborator, are exactly the case this describes.

The lightweight version costs nothing - write down what evidence you would want before
committing equity, and what evidence you can show. Better done before a conversation than
during one.
