# HELIX — Enterprise AI Execution Council (EAEC)
**Status:** RESERVED Future Architecture · **Reserved:** July 21, 2026
**Parent:** HELIX Workspace · Classification: Enterprise Governance & AI Strategy

# Purpose
An organizational framework enabling enterprises to systematically discover, evaluate, govern, deploy, and continuously improve AI capabilities. Unlike research-only AI think tanks, the EAEC transforms AI opportunities into measurable operational outcomes through structured governance, cross-functional collaboration, and production readiness.

# Core Doctrine
Organizations do not create value by experimenting with AI; they create value by repeatedly identifying, governing, deploying, measuring, and improving AI execution. The shift: from "what AI models should we use?" to "which AI capabilities should be admitted into production, under what governance, and how is success measured?"

# Structure
Permanent cross-functional operating capability: executive leadership, product, business ops, enterprise architecture, software/AI/data engineering, IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, legal, compliance, risk, finance, HR, customer operations.

# AI Opportunity Lifecycle
Business opportunity > intake > business qualification > technical assessment > data readiness > security review > legal & compliance > architecture review > pilot approval > production deployment > continuous monitoring > optimization > retirement or evolution. Every initiative follows an observable governance lifecycle.

# Intake Template
Business problem, current workflow, expected value, required systems and data, automation opportunities, human oversight requirements, risk profile, success metrics, estimated effort — value evaluated before implementation begins.

# Production Readiness Gates
Business value · technical feasibility · architecture alignment · data availability · identity & access · security · privacy · regulatory compliance · human oversight · monitoring strategy · rollback strategy. Only initiatives satisfying governance advance.

# Working Groups & Portfolio
Domain groups (engineering, support, marketing, sales, finance, HR, legal, manufacturing, healthcare, supply chain) own initiatives under enterprise-wide standards. Council maintains portfolio visibility: proposals, pilots, deployments, metrics, value generated, risk, lessons, opportunities.

# Success Metrics
Opportunities submitted/approved, pilot success rate, deployment rate, cost reduction, time savings, revenue impact, adoption, incident frequency, governance exceptions, proposal-to-production time. Outcomes, not novelty.

# Relationship to HELIX
EAEC governs organizational AI adoption; HELIX Workspace provides the environment; Pathway decomposes work; Doctrine Packs establish standards; OCG preserves knowledge; vLOID governs admissibility (VERITY trust, IAM identity, OROS coordination, DRIFT deviation monitoring). EAEC coordinates decisions; HELIX components execute, govern, observe.
