# RESERVE — Domain-Aware Capital Intelligence (DACI)

Status: Reserved for future research and validation. This reserve preserves
a HYPOTHESIS, not a committed product. No execution authority granted here.
Canonical home: iam-integrity/docs/reserve/domain-aware-capital-intelligence.md
Captured: 2026-07-25 (consolidated packet — the "Domain-Aware Capital
Routing" entry from the six-canonical list; seeded by evaluator-domain
compatibility, staging/reserves-2026-07-24.md #2)

## Purpose

Explore whether capital discovery can evolve from generic investor matching
into an intelligence system that routes founders toward evaluators capable
of understanding their specific domain. Core hypothesis: the scarce resource
is often not capital itself but **domain understanding**. Technically
sophisticated ventures may struggle less because investors are unwilling
than because the reviewers lack the specialized knowledge to evaluate the
opportunity with confidence. The question shifts from "who invests in
startups?" to "who can accurately evaluate this category?"

## Non-goals

Not an investor directory and not another matchmaking marketplace. It does
not assume the hypothesis is true, does not declare a product, and does not
rank evaluators by popularity or sector label alone. It must remain
evidence-driven and explainable rather than asserting the conclusion
("investors can't evaluate deep tech") without validation.

## Relationship to existing stack

DACI is the evaluator-side facet of a three-part capital picture, distinct
from its siblings:

    Capital Admissibility Framework  -> project-level: is the project
                                        admissible for capital at all?
    Domain-Aware Capital Intelligence-> evaluator-level: who can competently
                                        assess this specific opportunity?
    Governed Capital Eligibility     -> execution-level: should THIS
                                        execution be financed right now?

It could integrate with the governance stack without becoming a separate
product: IAM (identity continuity) -> VERITY (evidence verification) ->
vLOID (admissibility) -> HELIX (orchestration) -> DACI (capital intelligence
& evaluator compatibility) -> Receipts (why an introduction was generated).
SURVIVOR preserves evaluation history, relationship continuity, and
accumulated institutional knowledge across fundraising journeys.

## Activation condition

Do not implement until research establishes: whether evaluator compatibility
measurably affects fundraising outcomes; which signals are genuinely
predictive; how compatibility can be inferred without subjective assumption;
and whether such a system offers value beyond existing investor-discovery
tools. Until supported by evidence, this stays a research direction.

## Research dimensions

Candidate evaluation signals (all requiring validation): technical
familiarity (AI infra, autonomous systems, robotics, cybersecurity, dev
tools, market infrastructure, healthcare, industrial, energy,
manufacturing); market familiarity (exchanges, clearing, payments,
insurance, supply chains, enterprise, government procurement, regulated
industries); regulatory familiarity (financial, healthcare, privacy,
licensing, international); and observed investment behavior
(infrastructure-first, platform, deep-moat, long-cycle, regulatory,
enterprise, consumer). The aim is inferred competence and compatibility —
project characteristics -> domain knowledge required -> evaluator competence
-> probability of meaningful evaluation — not another popularity ranking.

## Doctrine

Reputation opens the conversation; competence to evaluate determines whether
capital can form around genuinely novel work. Preserve the hypothesis
rigorously and let evidence, not enthusiasm, decide if it becomes a product.
