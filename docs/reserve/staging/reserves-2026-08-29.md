# Reserve staging ledger — 2026-08-29 (Batch 3)

Working record of the third notepad-to-canon batch. Twenty-seven accumulated submissions
were reconciled against commit `1ad146d` and the 106 canonical files then present in
`docs/reserve/`.

This is a **process record**, not a reserve. It exists so a later reader can see what was
decided and why — including the submissions that were deliberately not canonicalized, the
corrections made mid-batch, and the instrument defects found in the reconciliation itself.

## Corrections made during the batch

These are recorded rather than smoothed away. A reconciliation that hides its own
corrections is not a process record.

### B2 — recovered by ledger accounting after a manifest omission

The two mechanisms of Representation-Aware Cognitive Runtime were dispositioned during
reconnaissance and then **omitted from the frozen manifest**. They were recovered only
because approval was made conditional on the ledger accounting for every submission
exactly once:

    submission → initial disposition existed → manifest omission
    → detected by ledger accounting → reconciled destination → final canonical owner

Cognitive Object Store → `invariant-precomputation.md`; Ephemeral Cognitive Tooling →
`intelligence-resource-governance-layer.md`. Appends went from 13 to 15. **Without the
accounting condition, two dispositioned mechanisms would have disappeared silently.**

### P14 — reversed from refinement to boundary

The Transaction-Gap Financing append was first drafted as a positive refinement —
use-of-funds decomposition with the doctrine *capital inherits the evidence quality of the
execution it finances*. Reading TGFP in full showed that would have widened the primitive
from `confirmed transaction → measured gap` to `historical demand → forecast →
replenishment`, which the reserve explicitly excludes as general working-capital
provision. **It was rewritten as a negative boundary case.** Use-of-funds decomposition
left the batch unplaced rather than being relocated to another capital owner on the
strength of being homeless.

### P10 — narrowed attribution claim

The obligation-preservation append first stated that a participant *has not breached*
where our infrastructure fails. The architecture cannot establish that. It was narrowed to
`INFRASTRUCTURE FAILURE ≠ EVIDENCE OF PARTICIPANT FAILURE`, preserving that any
independent participant obligation is evaluated from its own evidence — neither inferred
from our dependency failure nor excused by it.

## Instrument defects found in the reconciliation itself

The reconnaissance instruments produced four false findings before producing any true one.
Recorded because the same discipline the canon applies to evidence applies to the tools
that gather it:

| Defect | Effect |
|---|---|
| `lease` matched `release` | 32 files reported; the true count was zero |
| `common (control\|ancestor)` matched `single point of truth` | an ownership claim inferred from an unrelated sentence |
| two anchor phrases spanned line breaks in the source | one false abort, one false gate failure |
| a case-insensitive guard matched its own disclaimer | the gate fired on a sentence refusing the doctrine it forbids |

**An empty result is evidence only if the instrument is proven capable of producing a
non-empty one.** Every keyword sweep in this batch carried a control (`admissib`, 108–113
files) for that reason.

## Constraints preserved into canon

**No formula for `N_effective`.** IVCP's existing *do not prematurely lock to one number*
governs; the extension names the inequality and stops.

**No Trust Independence Ratio.** Reducing an endorsement graph to a ratio before
relationship semantics are understood would recreate the compression problem the reserve
exists to name.

**No invented Governance Distance levels.** The originating material specified a nine-level
scale and supplied two endpoints. The remaining seven are recorded as an open research
question. **UNKNOWN is a valid computational output** — a rule this canon already applies
to external claims, applied here to its own architectural history.

**`company success ≠ stakeholder economic outcome`**, with the originating acquisition
account recorded as an unverified illustration. No claim about any party's proceeds is
recorded as fact.
