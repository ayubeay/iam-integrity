# RESERVE — Emerging Product Pain-Loop Intelligence (EPPLI)

Status: RESERVED — research discipline. NOT an active build, not a product.
Captured: 2026-08-27.

## Core thesis

Startup directories, launch platforms, founder posts, waitlists, beta programs and
product communities are distributed sensors of unresolved market pain. The objective is
not to copy products — it is to observe what many independent builders are attempting,
identify repeated problem clusters, and reason *beneath* their proposed solutions to find
deeper structural pain loops.

## Loop

    emerging products → problem extraction → clustering → repeated-pain detection
    → journey/funnel inspection → failure/omission analysis → external pain evidence
    → structural root-cause analysis → existing-stack comparison
    → BUILD / EXPERIMENT / RESERVE / IGNORE

For each cluster: what pain is claimed; why now; what the solutions share; where they
stop; what failure or workaround remains; whether the missing piece is a feature or a
structural constraint; why incumbents have not solved it; whether solving the deeper loop
requires trust, verification, coordination, liquidity, identity, governance,
infrastructure or incentives; and whether we already possess a primitive that changes the
economics of solving it.

## Pain-loop depth test

    L0 Cosmetic · L1 Feature · L2 Workflow · L3 Coordination
    L4 Structural (trust, verification, liquidity, incentives, identity, governance)
    L5 Primitive (reusable mechanism across multiple markets)

Prefer L3–L5. Ignore most L0–L2 unless immediate commercial leverage exists.

## Evidence discipline

**A product launch is not validation.** Maintain separate evidence for:

    builder interest ≠ user interest ≠ usage ≠ willingness to pay
    ≠ retention ≠ viable economics

Confidence increases only as independent signals accumulate — complaints, search
behaviour, repeated workarounds, purchasing behaviour, existing spend, switching
attempts, retention evidence, failed incumbent solutions.

## Architectural fit check

Every sufficiently deep discovery is checked against the existing architecture before
proposing a standalone product. A market may appear to need "another AI agent" while the
actual unsolved mechanism is execution admissibility, verification finality, dependency
provenance, continuity or trustworthy coordination — something an existing primitive may
already address. This prevents product proliferation when primitive reuse suffices.

## Output

A compact intelligence receipt: observed cluster · repeated pain · current approaches ·
shared omissions · underlying structural constraint · evidence strength · existing
alternatives · our architectural leverage · potential wedge · defensibility hypothesis ·
BUILD / EXPERIMENT / RESERVE / IGNORE · reason.

## Doctrine

**Do not chase what founders are building. Study what their existence reveals.**

Emerging products are market probes funded with other people's time, capital and
attention. Their collective activity reveals where pressure accumulates — but the
opportunity may exist one or several abstraction layers beneath the products themselves.
