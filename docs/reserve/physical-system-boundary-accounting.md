## Purpose

A disciplined method for investigating physical systems whose observed behaviour appears
inconsistent with the initially assumed energy model — **including entirely conventional
engineering**, where the same accounting reveals parasitic loss, unexplained power draw,
or an actuator diverging from its commanded model.

Do not jump from `unexpected observation → impossible`, nor from
`unexpected observation → revolutionary physics`.

    observation → system-boundary definition → expected energy model → input discovery
    → instrumentation → accounting → residual → replication → classification

**An unexplained observation is evidence of an incomplete model before it is evidence of
new physics.** Equally, established theory must not be used to discard reproducible
measurements merely because they are surprising.

## Boundary accounting

For any physical experiment, explicitly define: objects observed · spatial boundary ·
temporal boundary · initial stored energy · gravitational potential · kinetic energy ·
electrical input · magnetic/EM input · thermal input · pressure/pneumatic · chemical ·
elastic/spring · vibration/acoustic · mechanical movement of supports · environmental
gradients · human intervention · control-system intervention · external computation ·
material entering or leaving · measurement uncertainty.

**A system is not "closed" merely because the visible apparatus appears self-contained.**
Hidden batteries, coils, magnets, motors, pressure sources, thermal gradients, vibration,
mechanical preload, environmental harvesting, software intervention and measurement
artifacts remain admissible hypotheses until excluded experimentally.

## Energy-accounting primitive

    E_observed = E_initial + Σ(E_external) − Σ(E_losses) ± measurement_error
    unexplained_residual = E_observed − E_initial − measured_inputs + measured_losses

A positive residual is **not** anomalous energy. It means only that the current
accounting model does not yet explain the observation within measured uncertainty.

    EXPLAINED · UNDER-INSTRUMENTED · MODEL-INCOMPLETE · MEASUREMENT-CONFLICT
    · REPRODUCIBLE-UNEXPLAINED · (only after rigorous independent replication)
      NOVEL-PHYSICS-CANDIDATE

**There is no direct transition from UNDER-INSTRUMENTED to NOVEL-PHYSICS-CANDIDATE.**

## Experimental loop

Observe without interpreting → define the boundary → enumerate every conventional
explanation → instrument (mass, position, velocity, acceleration, current/voltage, EM
fields, temperature, pressure, vibration, acoustics, structural movement, timing,
environment) → synchronize all measurements on one timeline → reconcile predicted against
observed → **adversarially search for missing inputs, attempting to break the
extraordinary interpretation** → replicate across operators, instruments, environments →
preserve UNKNOWN.

*Failure to explain something is not permission to fabricate an explanation.*

## Hidden energy-path discovery

Search actively for energy paths rather than testing only a preferred hypothesis:
battery → coil → magnetic acceleration; temperature variation → pressure change →
motion; ambient vibration → resonator → stored mechanical energy; solar → storage →
actuator; human preload → spring → delayed release; software animation → impossible
visual trajectory.

The research question: **what complete causal path accounts for the observed state
transition?**

## Causal physical systems reconstruction

Extension of the same method to reconstructing how an unfamiliar apparatus works:

    SOURCE → TRANSFORMATION → CONTROL → FIELD/TRANSFER → INTERACTION → OUTPUT → LOSS

with a parallel information path:

    SIGNAL → ENCODING → CONTROL → MODULATION → PHYSICAL EFFECT → OBSERVABLE OUTPUT

**Energy flow and information/control flow must remain distinct.** A low-energy control
signal may govern a much larger energy transfer without being its source.

Three boundary classes: **analytical** (what the experiment considers inside), **physical**
(shielding, grounding, insulation, vacuum, thermal barriers, containment), **control**
(what external signals can influence the apparatus).

Mechanism evidence states: `OBSERVED → MEASURED → INFERRED → REPLICATED → UNRESOLVED`.

A Tesla coil is the reference test case: the method must independently separate electrical
power, resonant EM transfer, high-voltage discharge, thermal and acoustic losses, and the
separate information channel producing musical modulation.
