# RESERVE — Organizational Separation Doctrine

Status: Reserved. Future activation. Architectural doctrine, not an
implementation plan. No execution authority granted here.
Canonical home: iam-integrity/docs/reserve/organizational-separation-doctrine.md
Captured: 2026-07-25 (the value is the principle of organizational
separation, not any specific "US + offshore" legal structure)
Activation trigger: formation of operating companies, fundraising entities,
tokenized networks, regulated products, or international expansion.

## Purpose

As the ecosystem expands, let organizational boundaries follow functional
responsibilities rather than placing every asset, product, IP, and regulated
activity inside a single legal entity. The objective is not complexity — it
is operational clarity, risk isolation, governance, and long-term
resilience. Extends the ecosystem's modular software philosophy into
organizational design.

## Non-goals

Not legal advice and not a fixed corporate structure. It does not recommend a
jurisdiction or entity form, and it does not call for creating entities
merely because they are possible. Note: this is an architectural doctrine
for organizing the venture — for any actual entity formation, tax, or
licensing decision, consult a qualified attorney and tax advisor; nothing
here substitutes for professional counsel.

## Core principle

Separate what is being built from how it is governed, commercialized, and
legally operated. Each organization should carry one primary responsibility.
Candidate layers, each of which may eventually become its own organization if
justified: Research, Development, Infrastructure (core execution, identity,
governance, verification, shared services), Commercial Products, Operations
(employment, customer contracts, support, revenue), Capital (investments,
partnerships, strategic ownership), and Legal/Regulatory (compliance,
licensing, jurisdiction-specific activity, regulatory obligations).

## Relationship to existing stack

Mirrors the software architecture: IAM governs identity, VERITY governs
trust, vLOID governs admissibility, OROS governs coordination, HELIX governs
execution, SURVIVOR governs resilience — and products remain independent
while sharing common infrastructure. Organizational separation applies the
same clear-responsibilities / minimal-coupling / defined-interfaces /
independent-evolution philosophy to legal and operational structure.

## Activation condition

Activate only when there is a concrete operational, legal, fundraising, or
regulatory need. A new organization should exist only when it provides
measurable advantage in governance, regulation, operations, fundraising,
partnerships, or risk management. Organizational complexity must always be
justified by operational value. Until then, keep this as doctrine.

## Future research (at activation)

Jurisdiction selection, holding- and operating-company structures, IP
