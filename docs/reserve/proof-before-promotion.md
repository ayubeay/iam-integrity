# RESERVE — Proof Before Promotion Doctrine

Status: Reserve Only
Captured: 2026-07-17 (signal: "I built a compiler that replaces transformer
attention" — the community's response was "no you didn't," because
extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence before belief)

## Philosophy

The more extraordinary an execution claim, the stronger the evidence
required before the claim may influence execution, investment, governance,
or reputation. The system separates: ideas, hypotheses, prototypes,
verified capabilities, production-proven capabilities. These are not
interchangeable.

## Evidence ladder

Idea -> Hypothesis -> Prototype -> Measured Benchmark -> Independent
Verification -> Production Deployment -> Operational Track Record.

**Claims inherit the confidence level of the highest COMPLETED stage.**

Instead of "is this exciting?", the architecture asks "what evidence
currently exists?"

## Integration

**VERITY** scores claims by evidence quality, never presentation quality:
reproducible benchmarks, execution receipts, independent replication,
production telemetry, third-party validation, operational history.
Marketing language never increases trust. **OROS** routes decisions by
evidence strength: low evidence -> observe/sandbox/benchmark; medium ->
controlled deployment, limited execution; high -> production approval,
autonomous execution. **HelixAtlas** visualizes capability maturity —
progression through the evidence ladder, showing what has been demonstrated
versus what remains experimental.

## Governance rules

Extraordinary claims require proportionally stronger evidence.
Demonstrations outweigh descriptions. Reproducibility outweighs anecdotes.
Operational history outweighs projections. Confidence increases only when
new evidence is verified.

## Long-term vision

Future AI systems should not reward the loudest claim or the most
persuasive narrative — they should reward the most verifiable execution.
Optimistic enough to explore ambitious ideas; disciplined enough to
distinguish promising hypotheses from demonstrated capabilities.

Deep connection to the ecosystem's oldest idea: many systems ask "can you
do this?" — this architecture asks **"show me the receipt."** Confidence
tied to observable execution, not reputation or rhetoric. (Lived precedent:
the Jul 16-17 OHLCV shadow evaluation — an exciting integration hypothesis
was held at "prototype" until evidence arrived, and the evidence said no.)

Sibling doctrines: Adaptive Execution Layer (evidence gates for behavior
change), Recalculation Doctrine (route flexibility under stable
objectives), Verification-First Development Lifecycle (staged item 12,
Jul 16 — generation cheap, verification the bottleneck).
