# RESERVE - Evidence-Driven Distribution Intelligence

**Status:** Reserved. Do not implement until a product has real acquisition data.
**Not:** a standalone product or a generic AI marketing agent.

## The observation
AI compressed the cost and time of building software. It did not compress the difficulty of
finding repeatable demand. Idea to MVP to excitement happens fast; where the first hundred
paying customers come from does not.

This project is squarely in that trap's path - substantial architecture, many reserves, few
users.

## What to make measurable
Represent go-to-market as structured hypotheses rather than founder intuition:

    product or offer -> target customer and problem -> discovery channel -> message
    -> acquisition action -> activation -> revenue and retention

Then record what actually happens. Measure channel quality by economically meaningful
outcomes, not impressions, followers, traffic or posting volume.

## No universal channel
    SoundKeep     DJs, Serato communities, producer communities, referrals, search
    WIRE          enterprises needing verified robotics data, and contributors
    API Connect   developers hitting provider instability, quota exhaustion, latency

Different products, entirely different signals.

## Integration
VERITY evaluates evidence quality and confidence in GTM conclusions. DRIFT detects when a
previously working audience, channel or message stops behaving the same. OROS and vLOID
govern autonomous distribution actions and spending. IAM controls accounts and permissions.
API Connect normalises advertising, marketplace, analytics, CRM and platform capabilities.
Commerce Sniper may consume distribution intelligence, but this stays a reusable capability
rather than becoming synonymous with it.

## Doctrine
Optimise for qualified economic outcomes, not activity. A channel bringing 20 relevant
prospects and 4 customers beats one bringing 20,000 visitors and none.

## The activation condition matters most
Do not build the GTM machine before there is GTM data. SoundKeep's first few real users are
worth more right now than a month building a theoretical marketing system. Their behaviour
becomes the first evidence it learns from.

Building became programmable. Distribution is becoming programmable too - but only if the
evidence is preserved rather than the spam automated.
