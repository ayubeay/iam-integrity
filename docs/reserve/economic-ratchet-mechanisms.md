# Economic Ratchet Mechanisms - Monotonic Capability & Security Accumulation

**Status:** Reserve only. **Created:** 2026-08-09.
**Doctrine:** research the mechanism, not the token narrative.

Do not implement, issue a token, modify $SURVIVOR economics, or promote any price-floor
claim from this reserve.

## Origin
A public DeFi project described a mechanism where transaction taxes on both buys and sells
allegedly increase an internal NAV or floor variable. The project is NOT adopted, endorsed,
copied or treated as validated.

The transferable question is the ratchet primitive:

    can legitimate system activity monotonically strengthen some useful economic,
    security, trust, reserve or capability state?

**A monotonically increasing accounting variable is not automatically a realizable economic
floor.** That sentence is the whole reason this reserve exists.

## General form
    legitimate activity -> measurable contribution -> protected accumulator
    -> stronger system state -> greater future capability, security or resilience

Accumulated states might include verification reserves, audit capacity, loss-absorption
pools, challenge budgets, infrastructure capacity, operator reputation, evidence density,
reliability thresholds, earned execution privileges, liquidity buffers.

The ratchet must represent something operationally real, not a number mathematically
prevented from decreasing.

## Never conflate
    mathematical floor | accounting NAV | protocol reserve | quoted market price
    | realizable liquidation value | redemption value

A protocol can maintain a rising internal variable while its traded asset falls below it,
unless a credible mechanism economically connects the two.

Twelve questions any future research must answer: what backs the floor; can holders redeem
against it; under what conditions; who controls the backing; can reserves leave; what
happens under sustained selling; under thin liquidity; do taxes create value or redistribute
it; how do LPs behave; can supply, liquidity or NAV be manipulated; does it survive
adversarial sequencing; is the accumulated state useful without appreciation.

## The stronger interpretation has nothing to do with price
    verification request -> fee -> verification capacity increases -> stronger evidence
    governed execution -> receipt -> validated evidence accumulates -> earned capability
    challenge activity -> contribution -> stronger audit capacity -> more resistance

The invariant: USEFUL ACTIVITY SHOULD LEAVE THE SYSTEM STRONGER THAN BEFORE.

## $SURVIVOR is not modified
Not authorization for a guaranteed price floor, transaction-tax tokenomics, buy/sell taxes,
automatic burns, reflection mechanics, speculative promises, or copying another protocol's
mechanism.

## Ecosystem placement
**Zircon** - which ratchet structures exist across finance, insurance, distributed systems,
reputation and security markets; which produce real capability; which are accounting
illusions. Engineering receipts, not investment recommendations.

**Backpack Engine** - where does value enter, where can it leave, who bears losses, who
receives transfers, does activity create or redistribute, does it cohere without growth.

**FORGE** - simulate sustained buying, sustained selling, alternating activity, liquidity
withdrawal, stagnation, whale concentration, rapid contraction, adversarial sequencing,
reserve depletion, extreme volatility, zero growth. A mechanism that works only under
favourable growth does not earn promotion.

**VERITY** - separate the claim "the floor can only increase" from the separate economic
claim "holders can always realize that value." The second needs independent evidence.

**vLOID / LITMUS** - a mathematically valid mechanism is not automatically admissible.

## Demand lesson
The originating example reportedly had substantial traffic and little conversion. Anecdotal,
but the transferable lesson holds:

    mechanism novelty != utility
    utility != demand
    demand != sustainable economics

## Candidate metric
Productive Accumulation Ratio - durable useful capacity created over economic activity
consumed. A research question forcing measurement of what the ratchet actually creates, not
a universal standard. High transaction count with no durable capability increase is not
compounding.

## Activation criteria
Only if repeated legitimate activity already occurs; directing part of it toward a durable
accumulator creates measurable benefit; the benefit survives without price appreciation; the
mechanism can be modelled adversarially; simpler alternatives were compared; ownership of the
accumulator is explicit; failure cannot create hidden insolvency.

## Grading
MEDIUM as a research primitive. LOW as a reason to create a token.

Preserve the mechanism. Do not inherit the narrative.
