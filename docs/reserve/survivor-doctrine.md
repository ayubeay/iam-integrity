## Purpose

Establish endurance as a property every module must embody, not a separate
security service. SURVIVOR is the structural integrity that lets intelligent
systems keep operating under stress. Every layer of the architecture should
be able to answer: can this survive failure, scale, adversarial behavior,
dependency changes, organizational growth, and years of evolution? If the
answer is no, the design is incomplete.

## Non-goals

SURVIVOR is not a standalone product, not merely a security or fraud module,
and not a bolt-on resilience feature. It does not define new execution
stages of its own. It is a cross-cutting design requirement expected of
IAM, VERITY, vLOID, HELIX, OROS, and every domain engine.

## Relationship to existing stack

Endurance runs through the full pipeline rather than sitting at one point:

    Identity -> Trust -> Policy -> Execution -> Verification ->
    Receipts -> Learning -> Adaptation -> Endurance

Each module carries a survivability obligation: IAM survives identity
changes; VERITY survives misinformation; vLOID survives malformed intent;
HELIX survives execution failures; OROS survives orchestration changes;
KONIGO Connect survives network outages; Commerce Sniper survives regime
shifts; Opportunity Evaluation survives incomplete information; HelixAtlas
preserves history through system evolution; VYREL survives long-running
autonomous execution; VYRE preserves human continuity across devices and
sessions. Distribution/reachability is part of survivability too — a correct
system nobody can find or adopt does not endure (absorbs staging note #4).

## Activation condition

Always active as an evaluation lens: applied at design and review time for
every reserve and every build, not activated as a discrete implementation.
It is the standing question asked of any new component before it ships.

## Engineering principle

When designing a component, do not only ask "does it work?" Ask "will it
still work after ten years of change?" Endurance is not a module; it is a
property expected of every module.

## Doctrine

Intelligence creates possibilities. Governance directs them. Endurance
ensures they remain useful. The system runs on SURVIVOR in every bone — the
engineering philosophy that every subsystem is expected to embody, aligning
the ecosystem's emphasis on continuity, governance, and resilient execution.
