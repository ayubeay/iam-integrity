# RESERVE — Extraordinary Claim Evidence Tree / Unknown-Preservation Test

Status: RESERVED — research + adversarial evaluation. NOT an active build.
Captured: 2026-08-27.
Child of the `proof-before-promotion.md` doctrine, which asks *when a claim earns
promotion*. This reserve supplies the operational method: claim atomization, source
independence, discriminating predictions, falsification receipts and UNKNOWN preservation.

## Origin and disclaimer

Prompted by examination of "infinite Earth" material depicting alleged territories beyond
conventionally mapped Earth.

**This reserve does not accept those claims as factual.** The artifact is useful because
extraordinary claims create a high-quality adversarial environment for testing whether an
intelligence system can distinguish observation, artifact, testimony, claim,
interpretation, inference, evidence, corroboration, contradiction, unknown, falsification
and verification — without collapsing those categories.

## Core problem — unjustified semantic promotion

    "I observed an artifact"
    → "The artifact represents reality"
    → "The represented thing exists"

    OBSERVED: a map depicts territory X
    DOES NOT IMPLY
    VERIFIED: territory X physically exists

The opposite error must also be avoided:

    NOT VERIFIED  does NOT mean  PROVEN FALSE

The architecture must preserve UNKNOWN when evidence justifies neither TRUE nor FALSE.

## Evidence tree

    CLAIM → CLAIM DECOMPOSITION → OBSERVABLE CONSEQUENCES
    → EXPECTED EVIDENCE IF TRUE → EXPECTED EVIDENCE IF FALSE
    → AVAILABLE ARTIFACTS → SOURCE PROVENANCE → SOURCE INDEPENDENCE
    → CORROBORATING EVIDENCE → CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE
    → ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS → FALSIFICATION CONDITIONS
    → ADMISSIBILITY → CONFIDENCE → CURRENT STATUS

Statuses (provisional): SUPPORTED · WEAKLY_SUPPORTED · CONTESTED ·
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE · UNKNOWN · DISCONFIRMED · FALSIFIED.

## Claim atomization

Do not evaluate a large narrative as one binary proposition. A single claim decomposes
into separable claims — that a document exists; that it has identifiable provenance; that
it asserts something; that the assertion corresponds to physical reality; that access is
restricted; that specific parties know. **Each requires its own evidence, and evidence for
one must not propagate to the others.**

## Evidence independence

Ten sources repeating one originating claim are not ten independent pieces of evidence.

    original → repost → reaction → article citing reaction → thread citing article

may be one evidence lineage, not five confirmations. Distinguish **source count** from
**independent evidence count**; the distinction must influence confidence.

## Prediction / consequence test

*If this were true, what else should we expect to observe?* The objective is not
rhetorical debunking but generating **discriminating predictions** between competing
models: where MODEL A predicts X,Y,Z and MODEL B predicts X,Q,R, identify observations
capable of distinguishing them.

## Falsification receipt

Before investigation preserve: claim · hypothesis · expected observations · alternative
hypothesis · disconfirming conditions · confidence_before · timestamp.

After: evidence observed · evidence missing · provenance · contradictions ·
confidence_after · status transition · reasoning summary.

**This prevents the investigator or agent from silently rewriting the hypothesis after
seeing the result.** Optionally pairs with external pre-outcome commitment — see
`prospective-claim-commitment.md` and `evidence-commitment-and-anchoring.md`. An external
witness proves the hypothesis existed before the outcome; it does not prove it correct.

## Unknown preservation

**UNKNOWN IS A VALID COMPUTATIONAL OUTPUT.** Systems must not be forced to manufacture
certainty. Insufficient evidence may terminate as UNKNOWN or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE rather
than being coerced into TRUE/FALSE, and an agent may abstain when evidence does not meet
an admissibility threshold.

## The central question

The Information Admissibility Governor should answer
**"what may this evidence legitimately establish?"** — not merely "does this evidence
exist?" That is more fundamental than ordinary fact-checking, and it is the boundary
against both gullibility and premature dismissal.

## VERITY relationship

Source credibility alone must not determine claim truth. A trustworthy source can be
mistaken; an untrusted source can occasionally provide authentic evidence. Evaluate
**source trust** and **evidence quality** separately.

## Signal Drift application

Scenario material may contain true evidence supporting a false conclusion, false evidence
supporting a true conclusion, credible sources making mistakes, unreliable sources
accidentally correct, and genuine unknowns. The challenge is not identifying "the fake" —
it is determining **what each piece of information actually establishes.**

## Doctrine

    PRESERVE THE CLAIM. PRESERVE THE EVIDENCE. PRESERVE THE PROVENANCE.
    PRESERVE THE UNKNOWN. TEST WHAT CAN BE TESTED.
    DO NOT PROMOTE INFORMATION BEYOND WHAT THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS.

Neither *believe everything unusual* nor *dismiss everything unusual*.

## Boundary

Do not build an investigation product. Do not spend resources on physical exploration
based on social-media claims. Do not create a repository. Do not modify production
systems. Do not treat extraordinary claims as established facts. Do not treat lack of
current verification as automatic falsification.

## Activation

Activate only when useful for Information Admissibility Governor testing, VERITY
evaluation, Signal Drift scenario design, evidence/provenance research, pre-outcome
witnessing experiments, anomaly-investigation methodology, or agent epistemic-safety
testing. At activation: choose one narrowly falsifiable claim, define competing
hypotheses, establish evidence requirements **before** investigation, preserve a
pre-investigation receipt, collect independent evidence, track provenance, actively search
for contradictory evidence, reconcile against predictions, preserve UNKNOWN where
warranted, produce a final evidence receipt.

## Extension 2026-08-29 — Correction-Induced Misconception & the Claim Transformation Chain

Status: RESERVED — DO NOT BUILD. Architectural refinement of this reserve.

### Why this belongs here and not in its own file

This reserve already models a claim's passage through transformations —
`original → repost → reaction → article citing reaction → thread citing article` — and
already names the semantic-promotion failure that occurs along it. It also already names
one direction of the error a correction produces: **`NOT VERIFIED` does not mean
`PROVEN FALSE`.** What it did not carry is that a **correction is itself a transformation**
which can install a new false belief rather than removing an old one. That is another edge
on a chain this document owns, so it is recorded here rather than as a competing parent.

### The mechanism

A correction is not a subtraction. It is an event with its own reception, its own
compression, and its own failure modes.

    false or overstated claim
    → correction issued
    → correction compressed in transit
    → correction received against different priors
    → NEW misconception installed

Common shapes:

    "X is unverified"        received as  "X is false"
    "X was overstated"       received as  "X never happened"
    "the study was retracted" received as "the opposite is true"
    "not proven"             received as  "disproven"

The last is the inverse of the error this reserve already names, arriving from the
opposite side. Both are the same defect: **absence of establishment treated as
establishment of absence.**

### Claim Transformation Chain

Where a claim materially influences a decision, preserve the chain rather than only the
current state: original assertion · each restatement and who made it · what was added ·
what was dropped · each correction · what the correction actually asserted · how it was
subsequently restated · the belief state after each step.

**Compression loss is the mechanism, at every step.** A restatement that drops a
qualifier, a scope, a date or an uncertainty produces a different claim wearing the
original's provenance.

### Sophistication bias

A more fluent, more technical or more confidently worded correction is not a more
accurate one, and is more likely to be adopted without evaluation. This is the same defect
`computable-accountability.md` names as *fluence is not authority*, appearing in the
correction channel:

    presentation quality  ≠  evidence quality — for corrections as much as for claims

### Consequence for evaluation

A corrected claim must not inherit either the original's status or the correction's
confidence automatically. It is a new claim requiring its own atomization, its own
evidence and its own status — including the option of terminating at `UNKNOWN`, which
remains a valid computational output here as everywhere in this reserve.

**Do not assume a correction improved the epistemic state.** Establish it.
