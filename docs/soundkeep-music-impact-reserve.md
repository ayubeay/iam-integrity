# RESERVE - SoundKeep Music Impact Intelligence (MII)

**Status:** Reserved.
**Urgency:** LOW. Depends on data SoundKeep does not collect.

## Purpose
An evidence-based measurement layer for an artist's, song's or catalogue's real-world
cultural and commercial impact - using multiple signals rather than labels like "one-hit
wonder".

Not a recommendation engine. A measurement engine with receipts explaining every score.

## Signals
**Longevity** years actively streamed, playlist presence, multi-generation listening,
catalogue survival.
**Cultural reach** radio history, DJ usage, festivals, events, sports arenas, film and TV
placements, social reuse.
**Commercial** sales, certifications, streaming totals, touring revenue, ticket demand.
**Creator influence** samples, covers, producer and songwriter influence.
**Fan engagement** playlist saves, skip rate, repeat listening, library additions, search
frequency.
**Catalogue depth** - rather than "does this artist have three famous songs", measure songs
above a stream threshold, songs with sustained longevity, and the percentage of catalogue
still actively consumed.

## DJ intelligence
Opening tracks, closing tracks, transition frequency, BPM compatibility, setlist
persistence, and eventually crowd response.

## Momentum
Evergreen, rediscovered, viral resurgence, seasonal, geographic resurgence.

## Explainability
Every score carries its reasoning - years of sustained listening, certifications, tracks
above threshold, touring activity, DJ usage, playlist persistence - rather than a verdict.

## What to avoid
Do not build around "one-hit wonder" framings. Those are subjective, vary by country and
generation, and exist to provoke argument. Expose objective metrics and let the evidence
speak.

## Grading
Interesting and distant. Most of these signals require data SoundKeep has no access to
today. The DJ intelligence subset is the only part reachable from current data, and only
once real sets have been played inside the product.
