# SOUNDKEEP — Music Knowledge Graph & Taste Continuity Layer

**Status:** RESERVED (Do Not Build Yet)
**Priority:** Future differentiation layer
**Reserved:** July 17, 2026
**Relationship:** Deep companion to Modules 2, 5, 7, 9, 10 in soundkeep-reserved-architecture.md

## Purpose
Do not position SoundKeep as another streaming service or another DJ library. Reserve the direction where SoundKeep becomes the knowledge graph and execution memory for music — relationships between songs, versions, artists, remixes, and performance history. The long-term moat is not the catalog; it is the accumulated intelligence surrounding the catalog.

## Core Principle
Music is not isolated tracks. Every piece exists in a living graph: originals, official covers, live versions, acoustic versions, extended mixes, VIP edits, festival edits, radio edits, remixes, samples, interpolations, mashups, DJ edits, performance history, cultural moments. Every relationship is a graph edge, not a text description.

## Reserve Module — Music Lineage Engine
Track > original > official covers > community covers > samples > interpolations > remixes > DJ edits > festival versions > collaborations > current evolution. Objective: preserve musical history as a connected graph.

## Reserve Module — Taste Continuity Engine
Learn from ownership and performance behavior, not only listening. Future receipts: added to crate, removed from crate, exported to DJ software, played live, mixed after Track X, mixed before Track Y, saved repeatedly, re-downloaded, favorited, skipped, archived. Taste becomes evolving execution history, not a recommendation model. (Natural fit with the vLOID receipts/decision-trace doctrine.)

## Reserve Module — Explainable Recommendations
Recommendations generate reasoning, not only predictions: similar harmonic profile, compatible transition energy, frequently mixed after Track A, common within preferred genre clusters, preferred by DJs with similar crate history. NOTE: Sonic's live "Why this" reasons are the v0 seed of this module — already shipping.

## Reserve Module — Musical Knowledge Graph
Edges: artist>track, track>remix, track>cover, track>sample, track>interpolation, track>genre, track>mood, track>BPM, track>key, track>festival usage, track>historical events, track>influenced artist, track>inspired work. The graph evolves continuously.

## Reserve Module — Cultural Timeline
Per-song living timeline: original release, album release, chart milestones, awards, viral resurgence, film/TV placement, major remixes, live collaborations, significant sampling, community milestones. Music becomes historical rather than static.

## Reserve Module — Community Knowledge
Structured observations as metadata: preferred original vs cover, DJ preference, radio preference, streaming vs festival vs wedding popularity, community sentiment, historical significance.

## Strategic Position
Do not compete with Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, or Beatport on catalog size. Position SoundKeep as the knowledge, provenance, and taste-intelligence layer complementing existing libraries. The moat compounds: relationships + performance behavior + explainable reasoning + cultural context.

## Future Integration Surface
DJ crate management, playlist intelligence, set history visualization, community taste graphs, performance analytics, discovery engines, AI-assisted set preparation, knowledge-graph search, explainable recommendation engines.

## Doctrine
Do not build now. Revisit only after the core SoundKeep experience has traction. This is a durable-moat direction that compounds over time, not an initial product requirement.
