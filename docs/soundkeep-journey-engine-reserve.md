# SOUNDKEEP — Listening Journey Engine

**Status:** RESERVED — Do Not Build Yet · **Reserved:** July 17, 2026

## Vocabulary guard (binding)
Pathway = the engine. Transition = one recommendation. Flow = the experience. **Journey = a future experience layer built ON Pathway** — never a replacement term for the engine. Sequencing: Core Platform → Sonic Intelligence → Pathway Engine → Musical Journeys → Knowledge Graph → Community Learning. Journey ships only after the Pathway engine is mature, so every journey reuses one intelligence instead of bespoke systems.

## The problem (not similarity)
"I've exhausted my relationship with one artist but don't want to lose the emotional connection." Streaming answers with more-of-the-same or lookalikes. Sonic should understand WHY someone loves an artist (introspective storytelling, warm production, late-night mood, BPM comfort zone) and expand the emotional space around them — one bridge song at a time, returning home: J. Cole → Little Simz → Saba → Mick Jenkins → back to J. Cole. Never abandonment; expansion.

## Capabilities (reserved)
- Fatigue detection: artist, genre, playlist, seasonal, emotional repetition
- Taste-preserving bridges: variety that respects the original attachment
- Journey products: Afrobeat Journey, 90s Hip-Hop Journey, artist journeys, Burnout Recovery Journey, Regional Discovery Journey
- DJ mirror: detect set repetition week-over-week; suggest adjacent regional sounds and overlooked releases with compatible transitions — freshness without changing the DJ's identity

## Dependencies
Pathway engine maturity; listening/performance history (taste-continuity receipts, see knowledge-graph reserve); the explainability primitive already live in "Why this."

## Doctrine
Do not build now. The roadmap through Sonic Intelligence and the Pathway engine comes first. Long horizon: Sonic evolves from recommending tracks to understanding how a person's relationship with music changes over months and years.
