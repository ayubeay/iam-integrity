# SOUNDKEEP — Moments & Timeline Intelligence
**Status:** RESERVED Future Architecture · **Reserved:** July 21, 2026
**Classification:** Music Knowledge & Discovery Infrastructure. Does not change Phase 1 (organize and own a permanent library); extends it later.

# Core Doctrine
People remember moments, not only songs: the guitar solo, the bridge, the beat drop, the chorus, the vocal run, the lyric, the transition, the emotion. SoundKeep preserves these.

# Timeline Objects
Bookmarks on the song timeline: favorite bridge/chorus/verse, beat drop, guitar solo, piano section, vocal harmony, instrument break, DJ transition, remix section, live highlight.

# Moment Metadata
Start/end timestamps, personal note, tags, mood, energy, instrument focus, reason for saving, private or shared visibility.

# Discovery
Natural search across personal music knowledge: "Show me every bridge I saved." "Find emotional piano moments." "Show my favorite beat drops."

# AI Companion (Sonic)
Recognize recurring musical preferences; surface forgotten favorite moments; recommend similar passages across the owned library; learn which kinds of moments resonate over time. Agent-native music infrastructure, not a streaming interface.

# Community Layer (optional)
Share a moment instead of a whole song: song > timestamp > personal note > share. "Listen from 2:14 to 2:32."

# Long-Term Vision
From "my music collection" to "my musical memory" — a knowledge layer preserving not only what users own but why each musical moment matters.
