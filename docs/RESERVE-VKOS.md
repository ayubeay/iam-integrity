# RESERVE - Vertical Knowledge Operating System (VKOS)

**Status:** Reserved. Deferred until core ecosystem traction.
**Urgency:** LOW. Depends on products that do not yet have users.

## Summary
Infrastructure that turns knowledge into verified capability, governed execution and
measurable opportunity. Unlike learning platforms that end at content or certification,
VKOS closes the loop: learning connects to execution, execution produces evidence, evidence
builds reputation, reputation unlocks opportunity.

Not a product. Reusable infrastructure several products consume once they have traction.

## Lifecycle
    intent -> knowledge -> practice -> assessment -> capability -> execution
    -> receipts -> reputation -> opportunity -> continuous learning

## Principles
Learning should be measurable - watching content is not competence. Capability should be
demonstrated, not passively completed. Reputation should come from verified execution
history rather than popularity. Opportunity should follow capability. Learning never
terminates; execution improves future knowledge.

## Layers
**Intent** goals, pathways, requirements. **Knowledge** docs, video, simulation, AI
tutoring, workshops. **Practice** guided exercises before real work. **Assessment**
simulations, projects, peer and expert review. **Capability** portable, evidence-backed
competency graphs rather than isolated certificates. **Execution** capability applied to
real work. **Receipts** identity, capability used, reviewer, policy version, evidence,
outcome, confidence, signature. **Reputation** consistency, reliability, quality,
specialisation, improvement trajectory. **Opportunity** capability-driven rather than
profile-driven. **Feedback** execution improves content, assessment and capability models.

## ShiftTrust as the opportunity engine
ShiftTrust sits between capability and execution - the matching layer.

    VKOS asks:       can this person perform the work?
    ShiftTrust asks: where can they apply it today?

Opportunity types should span one-time tasks, hourly shifts, seasonal work, contracts,
apprenticeships, internships, long-term employment and enterprise staffing - across
warehousing, restaurants, retail, manufacturing, agriculture, robotics, creative and
enterprise field work.

Matching asks who has demonstrated the required capability rather than who wants a job.
Every completed shift produces a receipt that strengthens reputation.

    learner -> certified -> entry-level -> verified -> senior -> supervisor -> trainer
    -> enterprise partner

## Ecosystem
Wire (robotics contributor onboarding), SoundKeep (creative education and collaboration),
Earthwise (agricultural knowledge and export readiness), and future products - all
consuming the same primitives rather than each rebuilding learning and reputation.

## Integration
Stewardship doctrine, PRAETOR posture, VERITY credibility, IAM identity and history, vLOID
admissibility, OROS orchestration, SURVIVOR signed receipts.

## Grading
Downstream of adoption that does not exist yet. Building it now would be infrastructure for
a scale nobody has asked for. The value is having the primitive described, so that when
SoundKeep or Wire needs education or reputation they consume rather than invent.

Revisit when one ecosystem product has recurring users and a reason to certify them.
