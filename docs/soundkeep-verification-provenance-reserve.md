# Reserve: Music Verification, Provenance & Collection Intelligence

**Status:** Reserved. No implementation.

Evolve beyond organisation into verification: help users understand, verify and maintain
trustworthy collections. Analyse local or connected libraries without uploading them, and
produce evidence-based receipts rather than pass/fail verdicts.

## Capabilities
**Audio quality verification** - detect probable upscales and lossy transcodes posing as
FLAC. Report codec, bitrate, sample rate, bit depth, dynamic range, spectral
characteristics and confidence.

**Provenance** - where a track originated, acquisition history where available, and the
distinction between original release, remaster, deluxe edition, live recording and radio
edit where evidence supports it. Never claim certainty that cannot be established.

**Version intelligence** - duplicates across releases, comparison between masters, and an
explanation of what actually differs rather than a metadata diff.

**Collection health** - duplicate files, corruption, inconsistent metadata, missing
artwork, tags, replay gain, cue sheets or lyrics.

## Receipts
What was analysed, what evidence was collected, what was concluded, confidence, the
limitations of the observation, and why a recommendation was made.

## Integration philosophy
Complement existing library tools rather than replacing them. Consume metadata and
analysis from other ecosystems; produce unified verification, provenance and
recommendation receipts. VERITY scores provenance confidence, IAM preserves ownership
identity, OROS orchestrates long-running analysis, VKOS turns verified knowledge into
governed workflows.

## Doctrine
Do not merely organise music. Help users understand, verify and trust the authenticity,
quality, provenance and history of their collections through evidence-backed analysis and
reproducible receipts.
