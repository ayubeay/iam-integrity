# RESERVE - SoundKeep Temporal Context

**Status:** Reserved. Not scheduled.
**Urgency:** LOW. One observed curiosity signal, not product-market fit.

## Concept
Understand a song in the world in which it was created, rather than interpreting everything
through today's.

## Observed signal
A public discussion about monetary references in lyrics produced many people converting
historical amounts into present-day equivalents and debating what those references meant
when the songs were written. A behaviour signal, nothing more.

## Possible capabilities
Then-to-now monetary values, purchasing power, original currency and geography, prices of
contemporary goods, slang and terminology, obsolete technologies, locations and businesses
referenced, cultural and historical events, social context, recording-era context, musical
influences, and the distinction between writing, recording and release dates.

    reference "$10", context year 1973
      nominal $10, modern equivalent ~$X
      but in purchasing power: a concert ticket, gasoline, minimum-wage hours,
      a typical product
    so the lyric's economic meaning differs from how a modern listener hears it

## Not an inflation calculator
The valuable capability is temporal interpretation:

    song -> lyric or reference -> WHEN and WHERE -> historical retrieval
    -> economic, cultural, social context -> explanation -> context receipt

Receipts preserve source, relevant date, geography, currency, inflation methodology,
assumptions, confidence, and the distinction between documented fact and interpretation.

## Placement
Part of SoundKeep's music knowledge layer - listener education, artist research, music
history, catalogue exploration, contextual recommendation. Separate from SaaS-squared, which
remains professional collaboration, production, licensing and rights.

## Validation rule
Accumulate additional independent evidence before prioritising. One interesting discussion
is not demand.
