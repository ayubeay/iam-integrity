# SOUNDKEEP — Inspiration Preservation Engine (IPE)
**Status:** RESERVED Future Architecture · **Reserved:** July 21, 2026
**Parent:** SoundKeep Creative Project Timeline · Roadmap position 9.
**Distinction:** The Timeline preserves what happened; IPE preserves where the creative spark came from.

# Core Doctrine
Creators do not think in files — they think in ideas. A five-second voice memo, a lyric in a notes app, a melody hummed while driving may become the defining moment of a finished song. SoundKeep preserves creative origins as first-class assets.

# Creative Seeds
Voice memos, melody ideas, hummed phrases, lyric fragments, chord progressions, rhythm ideas, spoken thoughts, ambient recordings, notebook photographs, short improvisations, emotional notes, inspiration references — every seed connected to the project that grows from it.

# Origin Tracking & Lineage
Voice memo > melody draft > demo > arrangement > studio recording > mix > master > released song. Preserved per seed: origin timestamp, source device, original recording, parent inspiration, derived versions, creative branches, merged ideas, final implementation.

# Branching Creativity
One idea, many works: a single voice memo may branch into Song A, Song B, and a chorus used in Song C.

# AI Inspiration Intelligence
Identify recurring themes; detect forgotten ideas related to current projects; recommend unused seeds; show origins; map project relationships; surface abandoned concepts that fit new work; visualize creative evolution across years. AI preserves and connects — never generates or replaces creative intent.

# Search
"Show unfinished melody ideas." "Find voice memos that never became songs." "Show every song that originated from this notebook entry." "Show abandoned choruses worth revisiting."

# Long-Term Vision
SoundKeep as long-term creative memory: preserving not only completed music but the ideas, experiments, and moments of inspiration that made the finished work possible.
