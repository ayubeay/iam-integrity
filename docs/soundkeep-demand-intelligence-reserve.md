# SOUNDKEEP — Demand Intelligence Reserve
**Status:** RESERVED · **Reserved:** July 19, 2026 (committed July 21)
**Scope guard:** SoundKeep does NOT commit to selling rare music. It becomes the intelligence layer that discovers unmet demand and routes users to legitimate sources — never assuming rights it does not hold.

# Core Thesis
Every day, music lovers publicly reveal what they cannot find: rare vinyl, out-of-print CDs, cassettes, regional releases, independent artists, demos, local music, live performances, collector editions. These are real demand signals. SoundKeep learns from them.

# Product Philosophy
Understand demand before supply. When thousands search for music that is difficult to obtain, recognize the pattern and help connect the ecosystem.

# Demand Signals
User searches, wishlist additions, marketplace requests, collection gaps, "looking for..." posts, collector discussions, artist requests, regional demand, genre communities, repeat searches. Demand becomes measurable.

# Long-Tail Music
Big platforms optimize for popular content. SoundKeep also understands the long tail: local bands, regional genres, independents, forgotten albums, small labels, historical recordings, collector and physical-only releases.

# Demand Score
Dynamic score per requested work: search frequency, wishlist count, geographic demand, marketplace availability, active requests, collector activity, community discussion. The goal is not popularity — it is unmet demand.

# Marketplace Intelligence
Collectors: find legitimate sellers, discover reissues, track availability. Artists: discover renewed interest, identify forgotten catalogs. Labels: identify reissue-worthy albums, measure regional demand, prioritize restoration. Retailers: stock what people actively seek.

# Rights Doctrine (binding)
Demand intelligence must never encourage unauthorized distribution. Prioritize connections to rights holders, artists, labels, licensed distributors, authorized retailers, verified second-hand marketplaces. Discovery and lawful fulfillment only.

# Community Intelligence
Public discussions, wishlists, marketplace requests, collector communities, user collections, search activity — respecting privacy and platform policies.

# Relationship to Existing Reserves
Extends Permanent Vault, Creator Library, Emotional Library, Ownership Layer. Those preserve music; this measures demand for music.

# Product Doctrine
Every search is a question. Every wishlist is intent. Every "where can I find this?" post is market intelligence — used to help creators, collectors, labels, and listeners find each other through legitimate channels.

# Long-Term Vision
Music demand should not disappear into scattered forum posts. SoundKeep evolves into the world's largest map of musical demand — revealing what people seek, what creators have forgotten, and where legitimate opportunities exist to reconnect music with its audiences.
