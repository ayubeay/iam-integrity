# SOUNDKEEP — Knowledge & SEO Engine

**Status:** RESERVED — Do Not Build Yet
**Reserved:** July 17, 2026
**Origin:** Distribution flywheel lesson — build the audience while building the product; the knowledge graph becomes the marketing engine.

## Vision
SoundKeep should not rely on advertising as its primary discovery channel. Every piece of structured music knowledge Sonic generates should be capable of becoming discoverable content — tens of thousands of genuinely useful indexed pages (genre guides, BPM collections, Camelot key collections, artist relationship maps, transition recommendations, festival playlists, regional music maps, new releases) instead of a few landing pages. Distribution through useful knowledge, not paid acquisition.

## Module 1 — Knowledge-to-Web Publisher
Convert structured intelligence into public pages: genre guides, BPM/key collections, artist maps, transition recommendations, festival and regional collections. Search-optimized while genuinely useful. (Renders the reserved knowledge graph public — intersects soundkeep-knowledge-graph-reserve.md.)

## Module 2 — Sonic Explainer Generator
Every recommendation can explain why the track fits, why the transition works, similar artists, compatible genres, historical influences. Explanations power educational articles and public knowledge pages. (Sonic's live "Why this" reasoning is the primitive — already shipping.)

## Module 3 — Community Knowledge Pipeline
Verified community corrections (genres, BPM fixes, key corrections, transition suggestions, regional classifications) flow back into both the recommendation engine and public knowledge. Early advantage: fast human support — a DJ reports "this should be 8A not 7A," it gets fixed quickly, trust compounds. Spotify cannot do this at their scale.

## Module 4 — Creator Education Hub
Transition tutorials, harmonic mixing guides, genre evolution, regional music histories, playlist-building strategy — internal knowledge becomes educational discovery paths.

## Module 5 — Sonic Citation Layer
Recommendations retain underlying evidence: community consensus, metadata, verified catalog info, historical listening patterns, editorial curation. Evidence trails power public explanations. (This is the vLOID receipts doctrine as public-facing provenance — same primitive as Distribution Receipts in the distribution-engine reserve.)

## Channel Doctrine (when activated)
Show up where DJs already are: Reddit DJ/producer communities, YouTube mix tutorials, TikTok transition clips, Instagram reels, SEO. Each piece of content points back to SoundKeep.

## Why Reserved
Priority stands: complete the SoundKeep experience, refine Sonic, launch Hero Drops, complete Pathways, ship the SK token experience. Only then expand into large-scale knowledge distribution. End state: every improvement Sonic learns strengthens both the product and its discoverability — a sustainable loop built on useful music knowledge.
