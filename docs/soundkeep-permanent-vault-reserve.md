# SOUNDKEEP — Permanent Vault Ownership Reserve
**Status:** RESERVED · **Reserved:** July 19, 2026 (committed July 21)
**Origin:** Users already purchase hard drives, SSDs, laptops, and phones to permanently keep their music. SoundKeep extends that ownership philosophy into the cloud rather than converting it into another recurring rental.

# Core Thesis
People are comfortable paying once to own storage (laptops, phones, SSDs, NAS, external drives) because ownership feels permanent. SoundKeep preserves that psychological model: the customer purchases a permanent home for their creative library — not rented space forever.

# Product Philosophy
SoundKeep does not sell gigabytes. SoundKeep sells permanence. The user purchases a permanent vault where their own music, recordings, projects, stems, lyrics, notes, artwork, metadata, and collaboration history are preserved. The promise: "Keep your music forever."

# Why this matters
Streaming subscriptions rent access. Cloud subscriptions rent storage. SoundKeep emphasizes ownership. A creator already owns their demos, stems, recordings, mixes, ideas — SoundKeep becomes the permanent home for those assets.

# Permanent Vault Concept (illustrative)
- Free Library: limited storage, local organization, playback, basic metadata
- Permanent Vault 100 GB: one-time purchase, permanent cloud vault, cross-device recovery, version history, collaboration metadata
- Permanent Vault 500 GB: one-time purchase, larger permanent archive
- Permanent Vault 2 TB: one-time purchase, professional creator archive
Purchasing additional capacity expands the existing vault rather than replacing it.

# What lives inside the vault
Audio (WAV, FLAC, MP3, AIFF, stems, masters) · Creative assets (lyrics, chords, BPM, key, notes, artwork) · Projects (DAW sessions, mix revisions, producer comments, version history) · Identity (credits, collaboration history, project timeline, release history) · Receipts (import, backup, recovery, integrity verification)

# Creator Psychology
Never "I am renting storage." Always "I purchased my permanent music vault." Stronger emotional relationship than ordinary cloud storage.

# Revenue Philosophy
Subscriptions fit continuously consumed services: AI assistance, collaboration, advanced search, metadata intelligence, producer matching, marketplace tools, analytics, team features. Permanent ownership fits vault capacity, archival storage, library preservation. Ownership and intelligence are separate products:
Free (organize local library) > Permanent Vault (one-time: lifelong creator archive) > SoundKeep Pro (subscription: AI, collaboration, matching, automation).

# Sustainability
Permanent cannot mean unlimited. Every vault purchase must be economically sustainable: predefined capacities, optional paid expansion, archival tiers, compression and deduplication. The business remains profitable while honoring the permanence promise. Pricing note: each vault sale must be priced with an endowment mindset — against roughly two decades of storage cost, not one year.

# Hard Constraints
- Users retain ownership of their files.
- Users may export everything at any time. No lock-in.
- Encryption by default.
- Permanent means preserved while the service continues operating responsibly — not an unrealistic promise of infinite free storage.
