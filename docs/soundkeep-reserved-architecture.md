# SOUNDKEEP — Reserved Future Architecture

**Status:** Reserved / Future Exploration
**Priority:** Low — do not implement unless explicitly promoted
**Reserved:** July 10, 2026
**Source:** Community research (Reddit) on streaming, discovery, ownership, artist tools, listener experience

## Core Observation
Users are not asking for another streaming service. Recurring frustrations: ownership, discovery, organization, community, artist connection, permanence, music becoming disposable. Preserve these directions without expanding MVP scope. Active wedge remains pathway discovery (Sonic).

## Reserve Module 1 — Music Ownership Layer
Personal vault, offline-first library, portable collections, cross-platform imports, ownership indicators, artist-approved permanent downloads, continuity independent of subscription.
Doctrine: users should feel they own their music experience even if providers change policies.

## Reserve Module 2 — Living Music Graph
Every version connected: studio > acoustic > live > demo > producer cut > extended mix > remaster > fan collab > official remix.
Doctrine: music evolves rather than being replaced.

## Reserve Module 3 — Playlist Intelligence
Nested folders, smart/AI/rule-based collections. By mood, producer, instrument, era, studio, collaborators, memories.

## Reserve Module 4 — Linked Playback
Album integrity, transition-aware shuffle, linked songs, required sequences, concept albums, live continuity.

## Reserve Module 5 — Music Knowledge Graph
Contributor graph beyond artist: producer, engineer, mixer, mastering, session musicians, songwriters, studio, equipment, location, influences, lineage.
Goal: discovery through people and craftsmanship.

## Reserve Module 6 — Artist Worlds
Production journals, behind-the-scenes, writing process, studio diaries, equipment, creative notes, version history, tour memories, community.

## Reserve Module 7 — Discovery Engine
Beyond genre: instrument, production style, tempo, rhythm, harmony, emotion, recording techniques, collaborator graph, hybrids, influences, listening behavior. Nearest to current Sonic wedge — likely first promotion candidate.

## Reserve Module 8 — Music Memory Layer
Songs optionally connect to life events, travel, concerts, photos, journals, mood, friends, locations, timeline.
Doctrine: music becomes memory.

## Reserve Module 9 — Community Intelligence
Discussion layer around albums, songs, genres, instruments, production, lyrics, theory, history. AI summaries surface themes without replacing human discussion.

## Reserve Module 10 — Collaboration History
Git-inspired lineage: original > edit > producer revision > remix > live > fan version > official release. Lineage without replacing originals.

## Reserve Module 11 — Interactive Albums
Branching albums, alternate endings, performance modes, audience-selected paths, seasonal versions. Not for MVP.

## Reserve Module 12 — Music Identity & Reputation
Identity infra for artists, fans, collectors, reviewers, curators. Verified contributions, reputation, curator credibility, trust. Intersects IAM/VERITY stack.

## Reserve Module 13 — Fair Creator Attribution
Transparent contribution tracking: credits, role, history, portfolio, discovery links. Potential royalty integrations.

## Reserve Module 14 — SoundKeep Atlas
Visual knowledge-graph exploration of artists, genres, studios, collaborators, influences, communities. Intersects HelixAtlas spatial patterns.

## Long-Term Doctrine
Not another Spotify. Direction: a living knowledge graph, memory system, community platform, and ownership-aware operating system for music — streaming is only one capability.

## Promotion Criteria
A module leaves Reserved only on: (1) validated user demand via the wedge, (2) differentiation incumbents cannot replicate, or (3) sustainable business value with a concrete revenue path. Until then: ship the wedge, validate with DJs, keep this file as the map.
