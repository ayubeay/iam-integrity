# SIGNAL - Ad-Free / Low-Cost Listening Access

**Status:** Observed demand signal, not a committed feature.
**Urgency:** LOW. Research question only.

## Observed
Listeners keep looking for ways to listen without both recurring subscription cost and
disruptive advertising.

## Research question
Can SoundKeep create legitimate models where the listener is not necessarily the party
directly financing every stream?

## Mechanisms to investigate later
Artist-sponsored listening windows. Creator or label-funded promotional access. Patron or
community funding. Non-interruptive brand sponsorship. Artist-authorised free catalogues.
Public-domain and Creative Commons catalogues. Access subsidised by other SoundKeep
services. Credits earned elsewhere and redeemed for licensed listening. Cross-subsidy from
future professional services where economically viable.

## Constraint
Do not frame this as "free Spotify without ads." Rights, royalties, licensing, territory
restrictions and sustainable artist compensation remain fundamental. Any model that works by
not paying rights holders is not a model.

## Principle
Separate who listens from who pays, while preserving rights-holder compensation.

## Validation
Continue collecting independent examples. Repeated evidence can later determine whether this
deserves its own access and economics layer. One request is not a market.
